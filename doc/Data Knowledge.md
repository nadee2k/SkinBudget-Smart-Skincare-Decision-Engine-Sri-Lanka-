# **Data Understanding & Relationships**
## Core Data Relationships
1. Brand → Product
One brand has many products
2. Product → Ingredients
One product has many ingredients
3. Ingredient → Concern
One ingredient affects multiple concerns

Logical Chain
>Brand → Product → Ingredient → Skin Concern
